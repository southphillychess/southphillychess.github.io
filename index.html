About Us

Welcome to South Philly Chess! We are a casual-play chess club that meets at Lucky 13 Pub in East Passyunk (1820 S 13th Street) every Wednesday night at 7pm. The club is completely free and open to all skill levels. Our members include new beginners and experienced tournament players. Boards and clocks are provided. Outside of regular club hours, we organize occasional weekend meetups, chess watch-party events, and tournaments. Stop by on Wednesday for an ice cold beer and a game! The night ends when we’re too tired to play. Our only ask is that you support the bar by buying a drink and/or food.
